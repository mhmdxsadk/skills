# Evidence and optional validation

Use when an evidence record is useful, as a brief section or separate artifact. Link an existing record rather than copying it.

## Decision and existing evidence

What decision is being considered? What evidence already exists, where did it come from, and which users or circumstances does it represent? Record dates and limitations where material.

## Critical uncertainty

Describe the assumption, its current basis, and what changes if it is wrong. State whether further validation is wanted, unnecessary for the next step, or deferred by choice.

## Proposed check, if needed

Describe the method, participants or source material, observation sought, stopping point, and how results would affect the decision. Record whether the check was performed and reference actual findings, if any. Record execution authorization separately when relevant.

## Findings and decision

Record actual findings, contradictory evidence, limitations, remaining uncertainty, and the recommendation. Capture the user's decision separately and identify any changes to the problem, scope, or next step.
