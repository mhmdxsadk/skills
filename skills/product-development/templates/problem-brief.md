# Problem brief

Use only sections that help the current decision. Replace prompts with supported content; record unknowns explicitly. Do not treat an empty field as permission to invent an answer.

## Context and decision

Describe the request, planning depth, decision owner, and decision this brief supports.

## Problem

Describe who encounters the problem, in which situation, what they are trying to accomplish, and the consequence of the current difficulty.

## Current approach and alternatives

Describe today's process, workarounds, existing solutions and the consequences of doing nothing. Explain what is known about their limitations.

## Desired outcome and success signals

Describe the improvement the user wants. Identify observable success signals, any agreed targets and baselines, or how missing ones will be established.

## Constraints and solution preferences

Separate fixed constraints from preferences and proposed solutions. Record their source and the implications for planning.

## Recommendation and next step

Recommend PROCEED, VALIDATE, REVISE, PARK, or KILL. Give reasons, unresolved risks, and the next decision or experiment. Record the user's decision separately when it differs from the recommendation.
