# Product and scope brief

Use the relevant fields below; reference existing material.

## Product concept

Describe the intended users, problem, desired outcome, and proposed approach. Separate the first release from any longer-term ambition. Reference the problem brief and relevant constraints rather than duplicating them.

## Approach comparison and choice, if unsettled

| Plausible approach | Fit with the problem and constraints | Main tradeoff or uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |

State the recommendation and its reasons, then the selected approach and decision authority. Omit the comparison for an existing settled approach.

## Prototype decision

Record whether a prototype is useful before the MVP and why. If proposed, describe its question, minimal scope, how its result would be assessed, and which MVP decisions depend on it. Record available findings and the readiness of dependent scope.

## First-release scope

Describe the first users and the core task they should be able to complete.

| Included capability | Why it belongs in this release | Material constraint or dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |

List relevant exclusions and deferred ideas with reasons. Identify deliberate extras the user chose after discussing tradeoffs. State agreed release success signals and unresolved targets.

## User review and open decisions

Record decision authority for scope choices, requested changes, residual uncertainty, and unresolved decisions with their dependencies.

## Recommendation and next step

State the recommendation, reasons, and next action. Identify what can proceed into requirements and what depends on an unresolved decision or prototype result. Keep evidence confidence separate from planning completeness.
