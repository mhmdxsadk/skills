# Product requirements

Use the relevant fields below; add identifiers only when references need them.

## Context and scope

Summarize the intended users, outcome, agreed release boundary, and platform or other constraints. Link the product-and-scope brief if available. Identify provisional scope and dependencies on outstanding findings.

## Requirements and acceptance criteria

Repeat for each capability or coherent group:

- **Capability and purpose:** Which included capability and user outcome does this cover?
- **Decision authority:** Source of the selected behavior, or the proposal/decision still open. Group shared authority at document level when sufficient.
- **Readiness:** Ready, provisional, or blocked; identify any prerequisite.
- **Behavior:** Who can do what, in which situation, and what result should follow?
- **Rules and constraints:** Relevant permissions, data handling, business rules, and quality needs. Distinguish fixed constraints from suggested defaults.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Observable successful outcomes and material failure or boundary behavior. Leave unsupported targets open.
- **Related workflow:** Reference its user/feature flow in the design brief or existing UX material.

## Open decisions and changes

Record consequential questions, the proposed answer where useful, and what depends on the decision. Capture user-requested revisions and any resulting scope changes.

## Readiness and next step

State what is clear enough for design and technical planning, and what remains provisional.
